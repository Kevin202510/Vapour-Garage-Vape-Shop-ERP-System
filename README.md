# Vapour Garage Vape Shop ERP System

The **Vapour Garage Vape Shop ERP System** is a comprehensive, centralized enterprise solution designed to streamline and optimize the operations of a multi-branch vape retail business. Built for scalability and operational efficiency, it integrates **Point-of-Sale (POS), Inventory Management, Supplier Management, and User Role Control** into a single unified platform.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Multi-Branch Management
The system supports centralized control and monitoring of all Vapour Garage branches:

- **Peñaranda Branch** – Padilla St., in front of New Public Market  
- **Main Branch** – Brgy. Rio Chico, beside Petron Gasoline Station  
- **San Leonardo Branch** – Poblacion, 2nd Floor beside Pot-Pot Pandesal  
- **Fort Magsaysay Branch** – Purok 5, Mapalad, beside Lho-Rhen Hot Malunggay Pandesal  

Enables real-time tracking of sales, inventory, and performance across all locations.

---

### 2. Point-of-Sale (POS) System
A fast and reliable POS system designed for retail vape operations:

- Real-time transaction processing  
- Discount and promotional handling  
- Sales reporting and analytics  
- Automatic inventory deduction  

---

### 3. Inventory Management

- Automated stock tracking per branch  
- Low-stock alerts and restocking recommendations  
- Batch and supplier tracking  
- Inter-branch stock transfers  

---

### 4. Supplier Management

- Centralized supplier database  
- Purchase order generation and tracking  
- Cost monitoring  
- Supplier performance evaluation  

---

### 5. User Role & Access Control

Role-based access ensures security and accountability:

- **Owner** – Full access and analytics  
- **Staff** – Sales and basic operations  
- **Cashier** – POS-focused access  
- **Inventory Agent** – Stock and supply management  
- **Developers** – System maintenance and configuration  

---

### 6. Reporting & Analytics

- Daily, weekly, and monthly sales reports  
- Branch performance comparison  
- Product movement and profitability analysis  
- Audit logs for transparency
